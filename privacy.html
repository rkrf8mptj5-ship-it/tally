Tally Phone-Down Allowance

Privacy Policy

Last updated · 24 August 2026

Tally stores everything on your device and sends nothing anywhere. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. We cannot see your data, because it never leaves your phone.

What Tally stores — on your device only

  • The tally itself: budget, rules, credits earned, focus minutes, payout requests and approvals, day-by-day history
  • Your settings: mode (family or just me), names you enter, daily goal, rollover preference
  • The parent PIN (family mode), stored on the device

All of this lives in the app's local storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

Motion data

During a focus session, Tally reads the device motion sensor to detect whether the phone has been lifted. This is processed on the device in real time and is not recorded, stored after the session, or transmitted. Outside a running session the sensor is not read. The system asks for your permission before the first session.

What Tally does not do

  • No account, sign-up, or login
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No data transmitted to us or to anyone else
  • No money: Tally never holds, receives, or transfers funds. Credits in the app are a record kept between family members; any actual payment happens outside the app, between you.

Children

Tally is designed to be used by a parent together with their child. Because no data is collected or transmitted by anyone, no personal data of any user — child or adult — is processed by us.

Purchases — when subscriptions launch

The current version of Tally is free and contains no purchases. If a future version offers a subscription, payment will be processed by Apple or Google through your store account, and this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe exactly what changes. Nothing a family has already earned will ever be taken away by a plan.

Changes

If a future version of Tally changes any of the above (for example, adds sync), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask for anything new it needs.
