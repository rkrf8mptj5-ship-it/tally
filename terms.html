1 · What Tally is

Tally lets a parent set a monthly reward budget and lets a child earn credits toward it through measured focus blocks (phone face down, motion sensor, three strikes). A solo mode does the same for a single user's own goals.

Credits are a family record, not money. They are not currency, electronic money, a voucher, or a claim enforceable against the app or its developers. Whether and how credits are honored is entirely a matter between the family members using the app.

3 · Family use

The family mode is set up and controlled by a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for the budget, the rules, the PIN, and any real-world settlement of earned credits. Children use Tally on a device and under supervision their parent decides on. The app requires no account and collects no personal data from anyone (see the Privacy Policy).

4 · Measurement, honestly

Focus blocks are measured by the device's motion sensor on a best-effort basis. Sensors differ between devices and situations; a block can occasionally end in a way you did not expect. Tally is built so that a broken block never takes away anything already earned — but we do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free measurement.
